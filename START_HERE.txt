================================================================================
                    PERSONAL LIFE OS - START HERE
================================================================================

Hello Rachel! Welcome to your Personal Life OS app. This file tells you
EXACTLY how to get it running in 3 steps.

================================================================================
STEP 1: INSTALL
================================================================================

Open your terminal (Command Prompt on Windows, Terminal on Mac/Linux)

Copy and paste this:

    cd [folder-where-you-saved-personal-life-os]
    pip install -r requirements.txt

Wait for it to finish. You'll see it installing Flask, TextBlob, and NLTK.

================================================================================
STEP 2: RUN
================================================================================

In the same terminal, copy and paste:

    python app.py

You'll see a message like:

    ============================================================
    Personal Life OS is starting up!
    ============================================================
    Open your browser to: http://localhost:5000
    ============================================================

Leave the terminal open (don't close it while using the app).

================================================================================
STEP 3: OPEN
================================================================================

Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.)

Go to:

    http://localhost:5000

You'll see the mind dump form. That's it!

================================================================================
NOW WHAT?
================================================================================

1. WRITE
   - Click the big text area
   - Write your thoughts, feelings, observations
   - No filter needed - stream of consciousness is perfect

2. RATE
   - Mood slider: How are you feeling? (1=bad, 10=amazing)
   - Energy slider: How much energy? (1=exhausted, 10=energized)
   - Optionally: where you are, key thoughts, ideas, urges

3. SAVE
   - Click "Save Mind Dump"
   - The app automatically analyzes your writing
   - A report appears with insights about your thinking patterns

4. REPEAT
   - Record as many dumps as you want throughout the day
   - Click "View Today's Report" to see patterns and recommendations
   - Click "View History" to see past dumps

================================================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES
================================================================================

✓ Your data is 100% private - stored on your computer only
✓ No cloud, no internet required, no tracking
✓ All your dumps are saved in a file called "mind_dumps.db"
✓ You can stop the app anytime by pressing CTRL+C in the terminal
✓ Data stays saved even after closing

================================================================================
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
================================================================================

"Port already in use" error:
  → Another app is using port 5000
  → Either close that app OR edit app.py and change 5000 to 5001

"Module not found" error:
  → Dependencies didn't install properly
  → Try: pip install -r requirements.txt --force-reinstall

"Can't find Python" error:
  → Python isn't installed or not in your PATH
  → Download from python.org and install it

Still stuck?
  → Read the README.md file in this folder
  → It has detailed troubleshooting

================================================================================
WHAT THE APP DOES
================================================================================

After you save a dump, the app:

1. EXTRACTS KEYWORDS
   Shows you what you're thinking about most

2. ANALYZES SENTIMENT
   Detects if your mood is positive, negative, or neutral

3. IDENTIFIES PATTERNS
   Looks for psychological patterns like:
   - Anxiety and worry
   - Perfectionism
   - Self-doubt
   - Procrastination
   - Overwhelm
   - And 5 more patterns

4. GENERATES RECOMMENDATIONS
   Gives you personalized suggestions based on:
   - How you're feeling (mood)
   - How much energy you have
   - Patterns detected in your thinking

================================================================================
EXAMPLE USAGE
================================================================================

Let's say you write:

    "I have so much to do and I keep putting it off. I feel like I should
    be perfect at everything but I keep making mistakes. I'm so tired and
    overwhelmed. Nothing feels good enough."

The app will:

Detect keywords:
  • do, perfect, mistakes, tired, overwhelmed

Analyze sentiment:
  ✗ NEGATIVE

Identify patterns:
  • Perfectionism (high)
  • Procrastination (high)
  • Self-doubt (high)
  • Overwhelm (high)

Give recommendations:
  • Break tasks into smaller steps - focus on one thing at a time
  • Remember: done is better than perfect. Aim for progress, not perfection
  • Your energy is low - prioritize rest, sleep, and gentle activities

================================================================================
DAILY TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS
================================================================================

✓ Write 2-3 dumps per day (morning, afternoon, evening)
✓ Be honest - the app can't help if you filter your thoughts
✓ Update mood and energy every time - these feed the recommendations
✓ Check your daily report before bed
✓ Review the history section weekly to spot long-term patterns
✓ Use the location field - notice how location affects your state
✓ Track your ideas field - you might notice creative patterns
