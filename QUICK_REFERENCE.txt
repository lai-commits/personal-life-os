MIND DUMP APP - NEW DESIGN QUICK REFERENCE
===========================================

NEW USER WORKFLOW:
-----------------

1. THROUGHOUT THE DAY:
   - Open app at http://localhost:5000
   - See quick form with 3 fields:
     * Text area: "What's on your mind?"
     * Mood slider (1-10)
     * Energy slider (1-10)
   - Type thought in 10-30 seconds
   - Click "Log Thought & Continue"
   - Form clears, thought appears in list below
   - Repeat as many times as you want

2. END OF DAY:
   - Click "Integrate & Review Today" button
   - App analyzes ALL thoughts together
   - See unified report with:
     * Mood range (your high/low/average)
     * Energy level (average)
     * Day summary (coherent narrative)
     * Emotional arc (mood progression through day)
     * Main themes (what you were thinking about)
     * Recommendations (2-3 personalized suggestions)

3. ANYTIME:
   - Click "View History" to see past thoughts/reports


KEY FEATURES:
-------------

* Collection Analysis: Thinks about your whole day together, not individual thoughts
* Emotional Arc: Shows if mood improved, declined, or stayed stable
* Main Themes: Extracts top concepts from all your thoughts
* Quick Logging: 3 fields only (was 7 before)
* Timestamps: Each thought automatically timestamped
* Live List: See all today's thoughts as you log them
* Mobile Friendly: Works great on phone/tablet


TECHNICAL NOTES:
----------------

* Database: SQLite (local, private)
* Backend: Flask with new /api/thought and /api/integrate/daily endpoints
* Analysis: analyze_collection() function analyzes thoughts together
* No external APIs needed
* No cloud, no tracking, no accounts
