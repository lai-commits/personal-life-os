# Deploy to Render (Internet)

Your Personal Life OS is now ready to deploy to the internet!

## What You Need
- GitHub account (you just created one ✓)
- Render account (free at render.com)
- That's it!

## Step 1: Upload to GitHub

1. Go to **github.com**
2. Click the **+** icon (top right) → **New repository**
3. Name it: `personal-life-os`
4. Click **Create repository**
5. You'll see instructions. Follow them to upload your files.

**Or I can help you upload the files.**

## Step 2: Deploy on Render

1. Go to **render.com**
2. Click **Sign up** (use your GitHub account)
3. Click **New +** → **Web Service**
4. Connect your `personal-life-os` GitHub repository
5. Leave settings as default
6. Click **Deploy**

**That's it!** Render will give you a URL like: `https://personal-life-os-xxxxx.onrender.com`

You can now access your app from anywhere:
- 📱 iPhone (on cellular!)
- 💻 Mac
- 📲 Any device, anywhere

## Timezone
The app now uses Hong Kong time (UTC+8) automatically.

## Notes
- Your app will be slightly slower on Render's free tier (5-10 seconds first load)
- It goes to sleep after 15 min of no use (wakes up when you access it)
- Upgrade to paid tier if you want it running 24/7
